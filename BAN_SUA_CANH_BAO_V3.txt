TRIPGUARD - BAN SUA CANH BAO V3
================================

Da sua cac loi khi test thuc te:

1. Radar co nguoi ngay sau luc khoi dong van duoc gui canh bao sau warm-up.
2. Neu mat ket noi radar, khong dung trang thai phat hien cu de quay servo.
3. Nut SOS PB1 va nut ACK PB10 co ca EXTI va polling du phong.
4. SMS dung GSM 7-bit, noi dung khong dau va so quoc te +84838740019.
5. Cuoc goi chi duoc tinh thanh cong sau "VOICE CALL: BEGIN".
6. Dung AT+CHUP de ket thuc cuoc goi, khong phu thuoc AT+CVHU.

LIVE EXPRESSIONS CAN THEM:

tripguard_radar_alert_armed
tripguard_radar_alert_count
tripguard_sos_button_raw
tripguard_sos_button_latched
tripguard_ack_button_raw
tripguard_sos_count
tripguard_alert_active
tripguard_alert_phase
tripguard_alert_last_result
tripguard_alert_last_success
tripguard_sms_sent_count
tripguard_call_started_count
tripguard_alert_error_count
(char *)tripguard_alert_last_response

TEST NUT:
- Khong nhan: tripguard_sos_button_raw = 0.
- Nhan PB1 xuong GND: tripguard_sos_button_raw = 1.
- Giu khoang 0.2 giay: tripguard_sos_count tang 1.

TEST RADAR:
- Sau khi cap nguon cho radar, cho 10 giay warm-up.
- radar_online = 1, radar_alert_armed = 1.
- Co nguoi lien tuc 2 giay: radar_alert_count tang, bat dau gui SMS.

TEST SOS TU THINGSBOARD:
- remote_command_count tang.
- alert_active = 2 trong luc xu ly.
- Gui SMS thanh cong: sms_sent_count tang.
- Bat dau voice call thuc: call_started_count tang.
- Sau khi xong: alert_active = 0, alert_last_success = 1.

Neu alert_error_count tang, xem tripguard_alert_last_response. Cac loi thuong gap:
- +CMS ERROR: 330: chua co SMSC.
- +CMS ERROR: 331: khong co dich vu mang.
- +CMS ERROR: 332: mang SMS timeout.
- NO CARRIER: mang/SIM/module khong khoi tao duoc voice call.
