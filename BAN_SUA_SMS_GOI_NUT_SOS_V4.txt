TRIPGUARD - BAN SUA SMS, GOI DIEN VA NUT SOS V4
================================================

Da dong bo chuoi lenh voi ban danang(4).zip da test hoat dong:

1. SMS dung UCS2:
   AT+CMGF=1
   AT+CSCS="UCS2"
   AT+CSMP=17,167,0,8
   AT+CMGS voi so dien thoai UCS2
   Gui noi dung UCS2 va Ctrl+Z

2. Goi dien dung:
   ATD0838740019;
   Chap nhan phan hoi OK cua modem, khong cho chuoi VOICE CALL: BEGIN.
   Ket thuc cuoc goi bang ATH.

3. Nut SOS PB1:
   Vua keo PB1 xuong GND la phat loa va xep SMS/goi ngay.
   Khong con cho debounce 80 ms.
   Latch van dam bao mot lan nhan chi kich mot lan.

Hai file chinh da sua:
   Core/Inc/tripguard_config.h
   Core/Src/freertos.c

Sau khi copy file: Project > Clean, Build, nap lai firmware.
